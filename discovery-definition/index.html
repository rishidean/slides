The handoff is the decisions. Not the chat, and not the prototype.

Part one

Discovery

Think with the model. Don’t ask it for an app.

What this answers: how a vague idea becomes resolved product decisions.
DISCOVERY · THE STARTING POINT

Every product starts as a sentence

“I want an app that helps people hail a pedicab. A rider should be able to say where they are going, request a ride, and know that someone is coming. It should feel quick and easy. We can figure out the complicated parts later.”

“Know someone is coming”

A spinner? A driver name? An ETA? A live map? A text message?

“Quick and easy”

No account? Immediate estimate? One-tap confirm? All of the above?

“Complicated parts later”

Payments, matching, maps, cancellations... which can be mocked without changing the product?

A team can agree with the sentence while imagining different products.

slides.rishidean.com/discovery-definition5
DISCOVERY · THE FIRST MOVE

Don’t ask for answers. Ask for questions.

THE WEAK MOVE
“Turn this into a complete product requirements document and design the app.”

Polished output, hidden guesses. The model invents users, policy, and edge cases; you never see which parts are its judgment instead of yours.

THE DISCOVERY MOVE
“Help me clarify this idea before we plan implementation. Ask focused questions until we can state the destination, primary user, desired outcome, and explicit boundary in two sentences. Recommend an answer with each question, but do not decide for me.”

The model interrogates; the human decides. Every consequential choice becomes visible before anything gets built.

Polish can hide the model’s guesses. Separate what’s known from what still requires judgment.

DISCOVERY · MOVE 3

Four ways to resolve a frontier question

RESEARCH
The answer exists in the world

Market norms, technical limits, prior art. Go look it up.

GRILLING
The answer exists in your heads

Focused questioning extracts and pressure-tests it. Most decisions live here.

PROTOTYPE
The answer requires seeing it

Build the cheapest slice that settles the question. Then discard it.

PREREQUISITE
Something must exist first

A task, not a question. Do it, then the question becomes frameable.

The skill is matching the tool to the question; most questions don’t need the expensive one.

Part two

Definition

Discovery removed the ambiguity. Definition preserves the agreement.

What this answers: how resolved decisions become artifacts agents can execute.
DEFINITION · THE SEAM

What crosses the seam. What dies there.

THE CHAT HISTORY
Dies at the seam

Exploration, dead ends, half-revised opinions. An agent can’t tell the decision from the detour.

THE PROTOTYPE CODE
Dies at the seam

Looks right, decides nothing. Carry it forward and you’ve adopted a codebase nobody chose.

THE RESOLVED DECISIONS
Cross, with rationale

What you chose, what you excluded, and why. The entire handoff, preserved as artifacts.

The decisions are the handoff.

Decision work is over; implementation work hasn’t started. Definition is the bridge between them.

slides.rishidean.com/discovery-definition17
DEFINITION · MOVE 1

Synthesize the agreement. Don’t dump the chat.

PRESERVE
The agreement

What was decided, what was excluded, and the rationale behind each consequential call.

REMOVE
The residue

Discarded paths, dead ends, the options you didn’t pick. Exploration stays behind the seam.

REVIEW FOR
Invented behavior

The model fills gaps helpfully. Every gap it fills is a product decision you didn’t make. Catch them here.

The LLM drafts; the human keeps the agreement honest.

DEFINITION · MOVE 2

A roadmap is ordered implementation intent

Not a list of ideas. It appears only after the agreement is coherent, and it holds at least two EPICs.

EPIC 1 · BASIC RIDER BOOKING
Fully specified
The workshop build

The complete rider-only path, decomposed into three ready sprints. This is what the harness executes in the next workshop.

EPIC 2 · EXCEPTIONS & REALISM
Directional
Where realism lives

Cancellation, no-driver timeout, real maps, richer matching, the driver experience, in-app payment. Excluded from EPIC 1 on the record.

A written EPIC 2 is what stops an agent from helpfully adding the future back into EPIC 1. An exclusion without a home reads as an oversight.

Every sprint names a demonstrable outcome

E1-S1

DEFINITION · MOVE 3

Eight fields make a sprint executable

Outcome

What demonstrable state exists when it’s done?

Scope

What’s included, and what’s explicitly excluded?

Behavior

What must the rider be able to do or observe?

Rules

Which states, transitions, and invariants govern it?

Dependencies

What prior state and artifacts may the agent assume?

Constraints

Which technical and product boundaries can’t be crossed?

Acceptance

Which observable conditions decide pass or fail?

Verification & record

What evidence returns, and where is the result written?

A vague field doesn’t save time. It delegates a product decision to a code generator.

slides.rishidean.com/discovery-definition24
THE READINESS GATE

What material product decision would the agent still have to invent?

If the answer is nontrivial, Definition is not complete. Find the gap in a document you can fix in five minutes, not in a codebase where the agent already decided for you.

YOUR TURN · 06
8 MIN

Repair a sprint spec

1

Open the exercise copy of a PEDAL sprint spec and walk the eight fields.

2

Apply the gate: what would the agent still have to invent? There are real gaps to find.

3

Repair it: write the missing decision into the right field, or trace it to the artifact that governs it.

DONE LOOKS LIKE
